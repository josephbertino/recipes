# Sweet Potato Casserole with Marshmallows

**Rating:** 4.7/5 (581 ratings)  
**Prep Time:** 15 mins  
**Cook Time:** 45 mins  
**Total Time:** 1 hr  
**Servings:** 8  
**Yield:** 1 (9x13-inch) casserole

## Ingredients

- 5 sweet potatoes, peeled and sliced
- ½ cup packed brown sugar
- ¼ cup butter
- 3 tablespoons orange juice
- Frozen cranberries
- 1 pinch ground cinnamon
- 1 pinch ground nutmeg
- 1 pinch ground ginger
- 1 (10.5 ounce) package miniature marshmallows

## Directions

1. Gather all ingredients, and preheat the oven to 350°F (175°C).

2. Place sweet potatoes in a large saucepan with enough water to cover. Bring to a boil and cook until tender, about 15 minutes. Remove from heat, drain, and mash.

3. Place mashed sweet potatoes in a large bowl. Add brown sugar, butter, orange juice, cranberries, cinnamon, nutmeg, and ginger; mix with an electric mixer until blended.

4. Spread evenly into a 9x13-inch baking dish. Sprinkle marshmallows over top.

5. Bake in the preheated oven until casserole is heated through and marshmallows are puffed and golden brown, 25 to 30 minutes.

6. Serve and enjoy!

## Storage

Store leftover sweet potato casserole in a shallow, airtight container in the fridge for up to four days. Reheat gently in the microwave or in the oven.

## Freezing Instructions

This casserole can be frozen before baking and without the marshmallows. Prepare the filling according to the recipe and pour it into a foil baking dish. Wrap the dish tightly in foil and freeze for up to three months. Thaw in the refrigerator overnight. Top with marshmallows and bake according to the directions.
